summary: EHR-integrated secure messaging is increasingly used for clinical communication, yet relatively little is known about how it affects how clinicians communicate with each other. In this study, we evaluated the relationship between secure messaging and telephone use among resident physicians. We found that more secure messaging use was associated with more telephone use, which goes against the hypothesis that secure chat might replace telephone use.
